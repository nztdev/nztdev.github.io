What is this place?
A living map of everything being built — projects, tools, and experiments grouped by what they do, orbiting in real time. Each node is something you can open, explore, and install.

How it works
There are currently — projects across 4 groups and 3 standalones. More are forming.
○
Dashed ring = group. Tap to expand it — its projects orbit inside. Tap the back arrow to return.
●
Solid ring = project. Tap to open the card — find links to the repo, the live page, and install instructions if it's a PWA.
∿
Resonate compares all projects by commits, stars, forks, or watchers. The field reorganises around the data — highest metric grows largest and drifts to centre.
◉
Grouped / All toggle switches between the clustered group view and every project as its own individual node.
